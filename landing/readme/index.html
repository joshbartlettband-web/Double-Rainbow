The panel

ControlWhat it does
KeysPlay the character. The horizon draws the sound's wave as it plays.
PRESETSThe next or previous character, of 30. In the codex, its pages.
GLO held, then PRESETSJumps five characters at a time.
KNOB 1 to 4The character's own four knobs.
EDIT heldThe knob cards show what each knob really moves.
ALGORITHMHow the keys play: SOLO, WEAVE, CAST, SCRY, TRACE (below).
SELECTThe key, a fifth at a time (WEAVE, CAST and SCRY). In TRACE, the song.
OCT- / OCT+The keys an octave down or up, two each way. In SCRY, the drill. In TRACE, the song.
PLAYThe character's beat, on and off. In SCRY, hear the question again. In TRACE, hear the song.
SEQThe next beat (it starts if it was off).
SEQ held, then SELECTThe tempo, 4 BPM a click, from 60 to 180.
RECTap the tempo. From the second tap the beat follows you.
ARPThe arp on, then its settings four at a time. Held most of a second, the arp off (below).
SCLThe codex: the Keeper's ledger, the engine's story, then something to try. PRESETS turns the pages, SCL again closes it.
HOME held, then ALGORITHMYOUR NAME, for the hello. PRESETS picks the letter, ALGORITHM moves to the next place, SAVE keeps it, HOME leaves it as it was.
HOME + SAVE held 3 sThe full Melodee synth, as it would have started. Nothing is saved, and Double Rainbow comes back at the next power-on.
MASTERVolume.

ALGORITHM: how the keys play

ModeWhat the keys do
SOLOEach key plays its note.
WEAVEStrum, like an Omnichord. A black key picks a chord and plays its root low; the white keys strum it upward from C3.
CASTA black key holds its chord while the white keys play a tune. The white keys follow the key SELECT picked, so the tune stays with the chords.
SCRYEar training: hear it, then find it on the keys (below).
TRACELearn a song. The next key lights up and you play at your own pace; a wrong key changes nothing. SELECT or OCT picks the song, of 14.

The black keys' chords

In WEAVE and CAST, each black key plays the chord of the white key just below it. These are in C; SELECT moves them all to another key.

KeysChords
F#3 to D#4F, G, Am, C, Dm
F#4 to D#5Fmaj7, G7, Am7, Cmaj7, Dm7
F#5Fm, borrowed from C minor (the bittersweet one)

SCRY

OCT- and OCT+ pick the drill, and SELECT moves NOTES and CHORDS to a new key. Three right in a row adds something new to tell apart. After two misses the right key blinks.

DrillWhat to find
NOTESThe key's home note plays (lit), then a mystery note. Play it, in any octave.
INTERVALSTwo notes, the first lit. Play the second.
CHORDSA chord of the key. Find the black key that holds it.

The arp

Every character has an arp of their own, on the beat. Press ARP to turn it on: the four knob cards become its settings. Each press of ARP shows the next four, then the character's knobs again (the arp keeps playing). Hold ARP for most of a second to turn it off.

GroupKnobs
NOTESSHAPE (up, down, bounce, walk, jump), RATE, RANGE (1 to 4 octaves), GATE
RHYTHMSTEPS and HITS (spread as evenly as they go), CHANCE, BURST (a fast double or triple)
CHANGESOURCE (KEYS, HOLD or AUTO, which plays by itself), SPICE, LOOP, EVOLVE
FEELSWING, HUMAN, ACCENT, LEAP (an octave jump now and then)
