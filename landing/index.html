Double RainbowFOR THE M-VAVE FM-1

24 sounds have gotten out of the Keeper's jars. Each one is a character, and each one is a real synth engine you learn by playing. It's free firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, for ages ten and up.

Try it in your browser Put it on an FM-1

No FM-1? You can still play. Use the keys on the screen or your computer keyboard.

Download the video (90 seconds) if you want to share it.

The story

Somewhere in the hills lives the Keeper, who catches sounds in glass jars and keeps them on shelves in a room with no light.

One night a small storm broke out of jar twelve. The jar shattered, and its glass walked off with the moonlight still inside it. A moth came to the light, as moths do. More of them have been getting out ever since, and nobody knows why the sounds are kept.

Every character has a page in the Keeper's ledger, in the Keeper's own voice. Read them and the pieces start to fit.

What's inside

  • 24 characters, each on a real engine. Vesper and Hermit run on a Prophet-5. Shard and Gloam are FM, like a DX7. Squall is noise, Wick sings vowels and Tock is an 808. Mote is an old game console, Glint plays the Casio CZ-1's own tones and Bog squelches like a TB-303.
  • Every character has four knobs with names like GLOW, BITE and HOWL. Hold EDIT and you see what's really under them, like CUTOFF and RESO. The synth words come along with the sounds.
  • Press SCL to read the codex. Each character gets three pages: the Keeper's ledger entry, the true story of the engine, and something to try.
  • The edge of the hill is the waveform. It traces the sound as it plays, so a square wave looks square.
  • Every character has its own beat. PLAY starts it, and the bass follows your chords.
  • The ALGORITHM knob is a spellbook of ways to play. SOLO is one note a key. WEAVE strums like an Omnichord. CAST holds a chord under your tune.
  • Chords show their names and Roman numerals, like Am and vi. Turn SELECT to move to a new key around the circle of fifths, and every chord moves with you.
  • SCRY trains your ear. Hear a note, an interval or a chord, then find it on the keys. Get three right and it adds a new one. Miss twice and the right key glows.
  • TRACE teaches songs one lit key at a time, at your own pace. There are 14 of them, from the Tetris theme and Hall of the Mountain King to Dies Irae and Für Elise.
  • The whole synth is underneath. Hold HOME and SAVE for 3 seconds to get the full Melodee firmware, with every engine and all 200 of Sequential's factory Prophet-5 sounds.

It also covers chords and Roman numerals, the circle of fifths, ear training for intervals and chords, and learning tunes by ear. It's free and there's no account to make. The browser version runs on most computers, tablets and phones.

Try it

In your browser

The same firmware, right in your browser. It works on computers, phones and tablets.

Open the demo Turn your sound on. You might need to tap once to get it going.

On an M-VAVE FM-1

Plug it in with USB and press Install. It takes about a minute.

Open the installer You'll need Chrome or Edge on a computer or an Android phone, and a USB cable. Safari and iPhones can't do it.

Is it safe? Can I go back?

Yes. The installer page has a "Return to official V15" section. You grab the official file from M-VAVE's website, and the installer backs up your FM-1 first. Installing firmware is always at your own risk. This is a fan project. M-VAVE didn't make it and doesn't endorse it.

How do I get to the full synthesizer?

Hold HOME and SAVE together for 3 seconds. You get the full Melodee firmware, just as it would have started. Double Rainbow doesn't save anything, so your own sounds and projects are safe. It comes back the next time the FM-1 is switched on.

Is there one for younger kids?

Yes. Rainbow mode is the little sibling, bright and made for small hands.

Double Rainbow is built on Melodee, Kerem Kilic's synth firmware for the FM-1. Melodee grew out of Felucca by Leo Kuroshita and Hügelton Instruments. Double Rainbow is a fork that adds its own world on top.

It's free and open source under the GPL-3.0. The code and the whole plan are at github.com/@REPO@. The characters are original designs.
