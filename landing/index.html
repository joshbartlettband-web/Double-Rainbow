Double RainbowFOR THE M-VAVE FM-1

Twenty-four sounds have escaped from the Keeper's shelf. Each one is a character with attitude, and each one is a real synthesizer engine you can learn by playing. Free firmware, for players from about ten up.

Try it in your browser Put it on an FM-1

No FM-1 needed to try it. Play the keys on the screen, or use your computer keyboard.

Download the video (74 seconds) to share it.

The story

Somewhere in the hills lives the Keeper, who catches sounds in glass jars and keeps them on shelves in a room with no light.

One night a small storm broke out of jar twelve. The jar shattered, and its glass walked off with the moonlight still inside it. A moth came to the light, as moths do. More of them have been getting out ever since, and nobody knows why the sounds are kept.

Every character has a page in the Keeper's ledger, in the Keeper's own voice. Read them and the pieces start to fit.

What's inside

  • 24 characters, eight engines. Vesper is a Prophet-5, Shard is FM, Squall is noise, Wick sings with formants, Tock is an 808 drum machine, Mote is an old game console, Murk is Casio-style phase distortion and Bog squelches like a 303. Glint plays Casio's own CZ tones, Hermit lives in a brass horn, and there are more: a badger, a woodpecker, a choir of ants, a spinning top and a living logbook among them. Each sound was made for its character.
  • Four knobs each, with honest labels. Turn GLOW, BITE or HOWL to hear what they do. Hold EDIT and the cards show the real settings underneath, like CUTOFF or RESO, so the synth words come with the sounds.
  • The codex. Press SCL for three pages per character: the Keeper's ledger, the real history of the engine (who invented it, and how it works) and something to try.
  • The horizon is the waveform. The edge of the hill traces the sound as it plays, so a square wave looks square and a bell rings out.
  • A spellbook of ways to play, on the ALGORITHM knob: SOLO; WEAVE, a strum like an Omnichord; CAST, chords under a melody; SCRY, an ear trainer; and TRACE, songs to learn.
  • Music theory you can see. Chords show their names and Roman numerals (Am, vi), SELECT moves the key round the circle of fifths, and every chord moves with it.
  • SCRY, the ear trainer. Hear a note, an interval or a chord, then find it on the keys. It starts easy, adds a new one after three right in a row, and after two misses the right key glows.
  • TRACE, songs to learn. The next key lights up and you play at your own pace. Fourteen recognizable public-domain tunes, from the Tetris theme and Hall of the Mountain King to Dies Irae, Für Elise and Carol of the Bells.
  • The whole synthesizer underneath. Hold HOME and SAVE for 3 seconds to get the full Melodee firmware, with every engine and Sequential's factory Prophet-5 sounds.

Plus chords and Roman numerals, keys round the circle of fifths, interval and chord ear training, and melodies to learn by ear and by sight. It is free, needs no account, and the browser version runs on most computers, tablets and phones.

Try it

In your browser

The same firmware, running on your screen. Works on computers, phones and tablets.

Open the demo It needs sound on, and you may have to tap once to start.

On an M-VAVE FM-1

Connect it by USB and press Install. It takes about a minute.

Open the installer Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer or an Android phone, and a USB cable. Safari and iPhones cannot do it.

Is it safe? Can I go back?

You can return to the official firmware at any time: the installer page has a "Return to official V15" section. You download the official file from M-VAVE's website, and the installer backs up the FM-1 first. Installing firmware is at your own risk. This is an independent fan project and is not made or endorsed by M-VAVE.

How do I get to the full synthesizer?

Double Rainbow starts every time the FM-1 is switched on. Hold HOME and SAVE together for 3 seconds to get the full Melodee firmware, just as it would have started. Double Rainbow saves nothing, so it never touches your own sounds or projects. Switch the FM-1 off and on to come back.

Is there one for younger kids?

Yes: Rainbow mode, Double Rainbow's bright daytime sibling, made for little hands.

Built on Melodee, multi-engine synthesizer firmware for the FM-1 (github.com/keremimo/melodee), itself a modified version of Felucca by Leo Kuroshita and Hügelton Instruments. Double Rainbow is a fork of Melodee and adds its own mode on top.
